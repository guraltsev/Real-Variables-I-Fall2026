      L8: The Vitali obstruction Tentative 2026-09-02

      Complete the Vitali argument. Enumerate the relevant rational translates of a representative set, prove that they are pairwise disjoint, and locate their union between a set covering and a bounded interval. Use translation invariance and countable additivity to obtain the zero-versus-infinity contradiction. Conclusion: no countably additive, translation-invariant extension of interval length can be defined on every subset of ; in particular, Lebesgue outer measure is not a measure on . Distinguish carefully between the claims ``not Borel,’’ ``not Lebesgue measurable,’’ and ``outer measure is undefined’’ (the last claim is false). Motivate the search for a large domain of sets on which the outer measure becomes countably additive.

      LN: Outer Measures, Section 3. Additional: Steif, Section 3.7; Cheng, Chapter ``Construction of measures,’’ Section ``Existence of non-measurable sets’’; Tao, Section 1.2.
